How this website handles your information

Information You Submit

Contact and catering forms collect the name, email, phone number, organisation, service details, and message you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and manage the requested service.

Storage & Access

Enquiries are stored in a protected database on our application server. Only authenticated restaurant administrators can view or update them. We do not sell enquiry data.

Optional Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you select “Allow Analytics.” It helps us understand aggregate traffic and device usage. If you decline, no Google Analytics script is loaded. You can clear this choice at any time by deleting this site’s local storage in your browser.
