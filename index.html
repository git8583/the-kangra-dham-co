Our Heritage

A sacred culinary tradition, brought to your everyday table

"In the quiet valleys of Kangra, food is not merely prepared — it is blessed."

Passed down through generations of master Botis (traditional chefs), Kangra Dham is a sacred feast cooked without onion or garlic, relying on pure ghee, fresh curd, and whole spices.

At "The Kangra Dham Co.," we bring this centuries-old culinary heritage to Noida. Every dish is slow-cooked in heavy brass Charotis to preserve its authentic flavor, aroma, and gut-friendly lightness. Whether you need a nourishing daily lunch or want to share a traditional Pahadi meal with your family, you are pulling up a chair to a genuine Himachali celebration.

👨‍🍳

The Boti Tradition

Hereditary master chefs whose hands-only technique, rhythmic stirring, and instinctive spice ratios have been passed through generations. No machines, no shortcuts — only sacred craft.

🏺

Brass Charotis

Traditional brass vessels that impart a distinct, irreplaceable flavour. Slow-cooked over controlled heat for hours — a technique over 500 years old that defines authentic Kangra cuisine.

🌿

Satvik & Pure

No onion. No garlic. Only yoghurt, asafoetida, ginger, and hand-ground masalas. Pure ghee and whole spices. Food as pure as the Himalayan mountains it comes from.

🔥

Slow Food, Served Fast

Each Madra simmers for hours in curd and ghee. Lentils soaked overnight. Khatta tempered with precision. This is slow-food tradition — delivered to you with speed and warmth.
